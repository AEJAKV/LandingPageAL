Questions

Who can apply?+

Residents of AB, BC or ON who are the age of majority in their province (18+ in AB and ON, 19+ in BC), with a steady income and an active bank account.

Will applying hurt my credit score?+

No. There's no hard credit check. Lenders may do a soft check, which doesn't affect your score.

What is a guarantor fee?+

If you don't qualify on your own, a third-party guarantor (surety) backs your loan so a lender can approve it. The fee is shown in your contract before you sign.

Can I pay it off early?+

Yes. Paying early saves on interest, and you may qualify for a rebate.

How fast will I get the money?+

Most applicants get a decision the same day during business hours. After approval and signing, funds are sent by Interac e-Transfer.
